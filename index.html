✦ Our Story

Crafted with purpose,
rooted in Pune

Sustainability by Saloni began with a single broken bottle and a stubborn belief that nothing beautiful should ever be thrown away. Today we're a small, passionate studio turning discarded glass and surplus cloth into pieces people treasure.

✦ What We Stand For

Our values

♻️

Zero Waste, Always

We never send anything to landfill. Every offcut, every shard, every thread is either used or responsibly returned. Our zero-waste commitment isn't a marketing claim — it's the foundation every decision is built on.

🤝

People Over Production

We work with local artisans in Pune who are fairly paid and deeply skilled. We believe slow, hand-made production creates better objects and better livelihoods. We will never scale at the cost of the people who make our work possible.

✨

Beauty Is Not Optional

Sustainable products don't have to look utilitarian. We obsess over the finish of every glass rim, the stitch of every cushion seam. If it isn't beautiful, we won't sell it. Sustainability and elegance belong together.

🌱

Rooted in Community

Our materials come from Pune's bars, tailors, mills and households. We give back by sourcing locally, employing locally and donating unsellable scraps to local schools for art programmes.

🔍

Radical Transparency

Every product carries a small label telling you where its material came from — which bar, which tailor, which neighbourhood. You deserve to know the full story of what you own.

🧠

Curiosity Over Convention

We are constantly experimenting — new bottle shapes, new fabric combinations, new finishes. If something has been discarded, we want to know how it can surprise us. Creativity is our most sustainable resource.

Small studio. Real impact.

We are not a large company and we never intend to be. Every number here represents a real bottle saved, a real piece of cloth given a second chance, and a real person in Pune who made it happen with their hands.

Our commitment to sustainability is not a trend or a selling point. It is the entire reason we exist. Every decision we make — from the bottles we choose to the packaging we use — is made with the Earth in mind.

  • No synthetic dyes used in textile finishing
  • All waste glass is returned to recyclers
  • Solar-assisted workshop lighting since 2023

✦ The Craft Behind Every Piece

From bottle to beautiful, by hand

Each piece begins with a carefully selected bottle — chosen for its colour, weight and character. Our artisans score the glass with a precision cutter, then use controlled heat and cold to make a clean break. Hours of hand-sanding and polishing follow until the edge is silky smooth.

  • Hand-scored and cold-broken — no power saws
  • Fire-polished rim for safety and elegance
  • Each piece individually inspected
  • Signed by the artisan who made it
🍾

Bottle Collected

Sourced from restaurants, bars and households — cleaned and sorted by type and colour.

✂️

Cut & Polished

Scored, heat-cracked, then hand-sanded through 6 grits and fire-polished to a smooth edge.

📦

Wrapped & Sent

Packed in recycled kraft paper with a card telling your bottle's story.

✦ The Story Behind the Stitch

From surplus cloth to something you'll keep forever

Our textile artisans work with mills, tailors and households across Pune to source pre-loved and offcut fabric that would otherwise be discarded. Each batch is sorted by weight, weave and colour, then washed and inspected before cutting begins.

  • 100% recycled and pre-loved fabrics only
  • Hand-cut and sewn by Pune-based artisans
  • Eco-safe dyes where re-dyeing is needed
  • No two pieces are exactly the same
  • Each piece carries a small tag with its fabric origin
